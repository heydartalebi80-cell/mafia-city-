
# Mafia City - Game Design

## 1. Game Overview
Mafia City is a 3D multiplayer social deduction and city management game for Android.

## 2. Supported Languages
- Persian
- English

## 3. Room Sizes
- 6 players
- 8 players
- 10 players

## 4. Game Roles
- Mafia: Eliminates a player during the night.
- Citizen: Discusses and votes during the day.
- Doctor: Protects one player during the night.
- Detective: Investigates one player during the night.

## 5. Game Phases
1. Lobby and player registration
2. Role assignment
3. Night actions
4. Day discussion
5. Voting and elimination
6. Win condition check
7. Next round or game result

## 6. Main Systems
- Room management
- Role management
- Chat and voting
- Player profiles
- League and rankings
- City development
- Coins, diamonds and VIP

## 7. Security
The server must validate all important game actions, role assignments and player rewards.

## 8. First Development Milestone
Build a playable local prototype before implementing online multiplayer.
